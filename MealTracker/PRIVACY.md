# Simple Foods Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 18, 2026**

Simple Foods is designed to keep your food diary and nutrition information private. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

## Information handled by the app

Simple Foods may handle the following information when you choose to use the related features:

- Meals, recipes, nutrition goals, personal foods, and app preferences that you enter.
- Barcodes that you scan or type to look up a food.
- Camera access while the barcode scanner is open. Camera images are processed on your device and are not recorded or uploaded.
- Apple Health data, if you connect Health. The app reads active energy and writes dietary energy, protein, carbohydrate, and fat entries for logged meals.
- Food and recipe files that you explicitly import or share through the iOS share sheet.
- Tip purchases that you choose to make. Apple processes the transaction; Simple Foods does not receive your payment-card details.

## Where your information is stored

Your meal history, recipes, goals, personal foods, preferences, and cached food information are stored locally in the app's container on your device. Simple Foods does not operate an account system or a server that stores this information, and it does not provide app-specific cloud synchronization.

Depending on your device and Apple backup settings, local app data may be included in a device backup managed by Apple. Health information is stored in and controlled by Apple Health. Information exported through sharing is handled by the people, apps, or services you select.

## Network requests and third-party services

Simple Foods contacts public food-data services only when necessary to retrieve or refresh food information:

- [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) may receive a barcode, language, or public food-record identifier.
- [The Swedish Food Agency's food database](https://www.livsmedelsverket.se) may receive a public food-record request.
- Apple may process optional tip purchases through the App Store.

As with ordinary internet requests, these providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, and the app's user-agent string. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of that information. Simple Foods does not send your meal history, recipes, goals, personal foods, or Health data to these food-data services. Pasted recipe text is analyzed on your device and is not sent online.

## Tracking and analytics

Simple Foods does not use your data to track you across apps or websites. It does not contain advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting services operated by the developer.

## Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as the app's local data exists. Deleting a meal or recipe removes it from the app's normal views; revision information may remain in the local database. Removing the app and its data removes the app's local database and preferences, subject to any device backups you control.

Data already written to Apple Health remains under your control in the Health app and is not necessarily removed when Simple Foods is uninstalled. Shared or exported files must be deleted from their destination separately.

## Your choices

Camera and Health access are optional. You can review or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings and manage Health data in the Health app. The app's core manual food and meal logging features can be used without granting camera or Health access.

## Children's privacy

Simple Foods is a general-audience nutrition utility and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected here by updating the effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the [Simple Foods GitHub repository](https://github.com/{{ site.github.repository_nwo }}/issues/new). GitHub issues are public, so do not include health information or other sensitive personal data.
